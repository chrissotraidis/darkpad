# DarkPad 0.1.0 — first public preview

**Prepared release notes; not yet published.**

Perfect Dark, native on iPhone and iPad, with editable touch controls, controller
support, optional artwork and a familiar ••• menu. DarkPad uses Dab's Mod
v3.15.1, built from the Perfect Dark decompilation and PC port through
rebelancap's Apple shell.

## Get your copy

Build a personal unsigned IPA on an **Apple Silicon Mac** with full Xcode and
its iOS platform. Keep **30 GB free after installing the tools**. Once the
release and catalog listing are live, [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint/releases/latest)
provides the guided build; the [README](../../README.md#get-darkpad) also has
the direct build command.

Install and sign your IPA with your sideloading tool, then import your own
**Perfect Dark (USA) Rev 1 / v1.1** ROM. `.z64`, `.v64` and `.n64` are accepted.
No ROM is read during the build.

The only release attachments are:

- `darkpad-v0.1.0-padmint.json` — PadMint's build recipe.
- `SHA256SUMS` — the recipe's SHA-256 checksum.

Source is available through the release's Git tag. No prebuilt IPA, game data,
compiled game code, saves or signing files are published.

## Included

- Native ARM64 iPhone/iPad app, iOS/iPadOS 15+, with ANGLE rendering through Metal.
- Editable touch controls, controller defaults based on GoldenPad, and touch
  controls that hide automatically when a controller connects.
- Native display, controls and audio settings, ROM validation, local saves and
  diagnostic sharing that asks before uploading anything.
- Dab's Mod v3.15.1 campaign, Combat Simulator, Ghost Trials, Randomizer and
  character customization.
- Optional HD textures and GE Plus content from files you supply.
- Experimental protocol-33 netplay with touch/gyro look and online chat.

## Preview limitations

The current engine has built, installed and launched on an iPad Pro with saves
preserved. Hands-on Perfect Dark and GoldenEye gameplay and controller navigation
on that build still await confirmation. Previous-engine iPad play and current
Simulator checks do not replace that check.

Simulator tests cover saved games, Carrington Institute, GoldenEye's Dam,
bot matches and Direct Connect against desktop Dab's Mod. Full campaign play,
iPhone touch usability, heat/battery use, sustained frame pacing and real-device
multiplayer remain unverified. Stay at 60 Hz initially; higher frame rates and
gyro aiming are experimental. LAN discovery requires an entitlement ordinary
builds omit; use Direct Connect. Xbox 360 art and the Goldfinger/TND64 add-ons
have not been tested with their optional content.

See [acceptance](../acceptance.md) for evidence and
[the player guide](../PLAYER-GUIDE.md) for setup and troubleshooting. Install
updates over the existing app with the same signing setup; uninstalling deletes
its local data.
